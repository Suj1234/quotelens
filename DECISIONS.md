- **2026-09-24 · Jev via OpenRouter (P2-T1; checked openrouter.ai/typesafe/jev-1.13 → 404 for the fetcher, then openrouter.ai/docs/guides/community/typesafe-sdk and openrouter.ai/blog/insights/what-is-jev).** Not the chat-completions API. Choice options are sent as keys `o0..oN` (text in the description) so long option strings never round-trip. Score answer (weighted mean of level index) mapped linearly onto our `{min,max}`. 8 s timeout, one retry on 429/5xx, any error or unparseable answer → Gemini emulation, failure logged to `model_calls` as `jev-openrouter ok=false`.
