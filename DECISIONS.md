- **2026-09-24 · Jev via OpenRouter (P2-T1; checked openrouter.ai/typesafe/jev-1.13 → 404 for the fetcher, then openrouter.ai/docs/guides/community/typesafe-sdk and openrouter.ai/blog/insights/what-is-jev).** Not the chat-completions API.
